Why everything I make ends up monospace

2026-06-20 · design · ~1 min read

Every time I start a personal project with a "real" typeface, I drift back to monospace within a week. I've stopped fighting it.

There are reasons, and they're mostly about honesty:

  • Every character takes the same space, so layout stops lying to you.
  • It reads like the tools I already live in — the terminal, the editor.
  • The constraints are freeing. Fewer choices, more writing.

It also ages well. A monospace page from 2005 looks intentional today. A trendy one from 2005 looks like 2005.

Constraints don't limit the work. They are the work.

So here we are, in Monaco and its fallbacks, and I don't plan on leaving.
