how to use the roster

campaigns

Lead with influencers 01–05. Drop a customer into the frame for "the family" group shots. Rotate so no single face owns the feed.

UGC-style content

Customers 06–20 power testimonial reels, "spotted at the shop" carousels, customer-of-the-month, gift-guide features, before/after tattoo healing posts.

pairings worth shooting

Wren + Sol — boss + bestie mirror selfie.

consistency rules

Generate one hero shot per character. Save the reference. Use --cref / IP-Adapter Face / image upload on every subsequent shot. Keep the Portra-400 film treatment across the whole roster so the brand feels unified.
