## How to use this portfolio

### For brand campaigns
- **Lead with influencers (01–05)** for new product drops, the website hero, paid social.
- Pair an influencer with a couple of customers for "the family" group shots.

### For UGC-style "real people" content
- Use customers (06–20) for: testimonial reels, "spotted at the shop" carousels, customer-of-the-month, before/after tattoo healing posts, gift-guide "what's in their cart" features.
- Rotate so the same face doesn't show up every week — the *roster* is the brand asset.

### Consistency across the roster
- Generate one **hero image per character** first. Save it. Reference it (`--cref` in MJ, IP-Adapter Face in SD, image upload in DALL·E) for every subsequent shot.
- Keep the same lighting/film treatment across the whole portfolio for visual unity (Kodak Portra 400, natural light, candid framing — same direction as in `prompts.md`).
- Mix and match settings from `prompts.md` with these character anchors. *Wren in setting D* becomes *Sol in setting D* by swapping the anchor block.

### Pairings worth building
- **Wren + Sol** — boss + best friend energy. Mirror selfie together.
